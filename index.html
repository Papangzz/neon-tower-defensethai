📖 HOW TO PLAY
🏰
Select a tower from the sidebar panel on the right
🖱️
Click on the grid to place the selected tower (green = ok, red = blocked)
🌊
Click ▶▶ NEXT WAVE in the top bar to start sending enemies
👾
Prevent enemies from reaching the EXIT (red arrow). Each leak costs 1 life
💰
Defeating enemies earns gold. Use gold to place or upgrade towers
⬆️
Click a placed tower to Upgrade (max Lv.3) or Sell it back
🏆
Survive all waves to achieve VICTORY! Casual = 20 waves, Medium = 30 waves.
